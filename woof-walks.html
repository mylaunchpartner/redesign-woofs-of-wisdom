More than a stroll in the park

Woof Walks are led by Alora Tawoosi, ABCDT-certified owner and operator. She brings her expertise in basic and advanced obedience, redirection and confidence building to every walk, so your dog reinforces their training and picks up new skills along the way.

Each walk is tailored to your dog's personality and learning pace, keeping them physically active, mentally stimulated and well-mannered.

A Woof Walk is a good fit if

  • You need someone to walk your dog during the day
  • Your dog struggles with reactivity
  • Your dog needs more enrichment or physical activity
  • You want walks that keep up the work from training sessions
