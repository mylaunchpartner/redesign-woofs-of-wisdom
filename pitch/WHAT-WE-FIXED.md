# What we fixed: Woofs of Wisdom Dog Training

- **Says what you do in the first second.** The old homepage opened with "Welcome to Woofs of Wisdom Dog Training!" The new one leads with what you offer, who it's for and where: private training and training walks with a certified trainer in Northern Virginia.
- **Prices and programs on the homepage.** Visitors had to click into another page to learn what you offer. Now the Puppy Package, private sessions, day training and mix-and-match, plus your session prices, are right on the homepage, so people arrive at the consult call already informed.
- **One tap to call.** Your phone number was plain text. Now every phone number dials when tapped, and on phones a call and consult bar stays at the bottom of the screen.
- **A clear next step everywhere.** "Book a free phone consult" appears in the header, the hero and the closing section, with the same wording each time.
- **Your photos doing the selling.** Your dogs, your training sessions and your walks now set the tone, with a photo gallery and a "Meet Alora" section that tells your story in a few lines.
- **Easier to scan.** Short sections with clear headings replace long paragraphs, and key facts (all breeds and ages, training where you are, the LIMA approach, your service area) sit in one strip near the top.
- **Better for search and accessibility.** The page now has one main heading instead of two, descriptive text on every photo, visible keyboard focus and responsive images that load fast on phones.

Your logo, purple brand color and voice all stay. It still feels like Woofs of Wisdom, just sharper.
