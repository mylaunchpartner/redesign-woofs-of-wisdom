Our training philosophy

Alora is a graduate of Animal Behavior College, and her approach is rooted in the science of animal behavior. It's built on operant conditioning: rewarding the behavior you want and guiding your dog away from the behavior you don't, in a positive, low-stress setting.

Just as important is how we communicate with our dogs. Miscommunication or the wrong method can set training back, or worse. So Alora teaches the why and the how, not just the what, so you can keep it up long after your sessions end.

The LIMA approach

Least intrusive, minimally aversive. Every training situation starts with the gentlest, least invasive methods, moving up the scale only when the situation calls for it. The goal is a bond of trust and respect, never fear or submission.
