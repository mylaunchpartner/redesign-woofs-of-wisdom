# Woofs of Wisdom Dog Training: concept redesign

A concept redesign (homepage, About, Dog Training, Woof Walks and Contact) by My Launch Partner for Woofs of Wisdom Dog Training (Alora Tawoosi, Northern Virginia).

**This is not the official site.** The real site is https://www.woofsofwisdomdogtraining.com/. Every page is marked `noindex` and each page's canonical URL points to the matching page on the real site.

- All facts, prices, hours, contact details and client reviews come from the current site. Nothing was invented.
- All photos and the logo are the business's own, taken from its current site. No stock photos were used.
- The free consult form stays on the real site, so every "Book a free phone consult" button links there.
- Every other link goes to another concept page, the real site, a `tel:` link or a `mailto:` link.
- `pitch/` holds before/after images for each page and `WHAT-WE-FIXED.md`. `pitch/case-study/` holds full-page before and after captures for the MLP portfolio.
