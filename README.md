# Woofs of Wisdom Dog Training: concept redesign

A homepage concept by My Launch Partner for Woofs of Wisdom Dog Training (Alora Tawoosi, Northern Virginia).

**This is not the official site.** The real site is https://www.woofsofwisdomdogtraining.com/. The page is marked `noindex` and its canonical URL points to the real homepage.

- All facts, prices, hours and contact details come from the current site. Nothing was invented.
- All photos and the logo are the business's own, taken from its current site. No stock photos were used.
- Every button and link goes to the real site, a `tel:` link or a `mailto:` link.
- `pitch/` holds before/after images and `WHAT-WE-FIXED.md`. `pitch/case-study/` holds full-page before and after captures for the MLP portfolio.
