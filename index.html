Meet Alora

“As someone who was raised part of a 15+ dog pack family, I learned to speak ‘dog’ pretty early.”

Alora Tawoosi grew up doing rescue work with her mom, fostering hundreds of dogs of every breed. Her mom has run Bark 'n Bubbles, a grooming studio in Leesburg, for 15 years.

Today she's a certified trainer through Animal Behavior College, specializing in basic and advanced obedience, teaching group classes and canine nutrition. Her whole program is built on communication, so you learn not only how to work with your dog, but why it works.
